Section 2 — Problem Identification

Primary Business Problems & Operational Friction

❌ Problem 1: Pricing Errors

Contract prices for corporate accounts are stored in disconnected Excel sheets. Manual copy-pasting into quotes causes a 5–8% error rate on customer invoices monthly.

❌ Problem 2: Post-Order Stockouts

Sales staff lack real-time stock visibility. Confirming orders via phone calls or daily reports leads to 3–4 stockouts per week after order confirmation.

❌ Problem 3: Credit Disputes

Accounting spends 15–20 hours/week manually cross-checking paper Delivery Notes and SOs. Overdue credit holds are bypassable via informal verbal appeals.

⚠️ Key AS-IS Bottlenecks
  • Sales Silo: Prices looked up in disconnected Excel files per Sales Rep.
  • Warehouse Lag: Goods receiving takes 24–48 hours before stock entry.
  • Purchasing Delay: Vendor RFQ comparison done via emails (takes 2–3 days).

📉 Operational Impact
  • Client contract pricing disputes on 5–8% of invoices.
  • Order cancellations and delivery delays due to depleted stock.
  • Excess purchasing of slow-moving items (`ST-010`) while paper (`ST-001`) runs out.
  • Delayed revenue collection and unmonitored credit risks (> 30 days).
Section 3 — Root Cause Analysis

5-Whys & Root Cause Breakdown

🔍 5-Whys Breakdown: Contract Pricing Errors
  1. Why 1? Sales Reps apply wrong contract unit prices on orders. ➔ Because prices are copy-pasted manually from Excel files.
  2. Why 2? Why are prices in disconnected Excel files? ➔ Because each Rep maintains their own client price lists.
  3. Why 3? Why are pricelists updated independently? ➔ Because framework contracts are filed as scanned PDFs in shared drives.
  4. Why 4? Why is there no central pricing engine? ➔ Because the company lacks a unified database mapping clients to contracts.
  5. Why 5? (Root Cause): Lack of a centralized contract-based pricing engine in ERP that enforces contract price rules automatically.

Section 4 — System Specifications

Enterprise Use Case Specifications

UC-01: Create Sales Order with Contract Price
  • Actor: B2B Sales Representative
  • Precondition: Client account & Contract agreement active.
  • Main Flow: 1. Select Client ➔ 2. System applies Contract Pricelist ➔ 3. Select SKU (`ST-001`) ➔ 4. System checks ATP Stock ➔ 5. Confirm Sales Order.
  • Exception Flow: Overdue debt > 30 days ➔ System triggers Credit Hold alert; requires Accounting release.
UC-03: Generate Purchase Requisition
  • Actor: Purchasing Officer / Warehouse Keeper
  • Precondition: Inventory falls below minimum reorder point.
  • Main Flow: 1. System flags low stock ➔ 2. Purchasing drafts Purchase Requisition ➔ 3. Send RFQs to Suppliers ➔ 4. Select Supplier & Convert to PO.
Section 4 — Business Governance

Business Rules & Non-Functional Requirements

⚖️ Key Business Rules (BR)
  • BR-01 (Contract Pricing Lock): System forces contract unit price; manual discount > 5% requires Sales Manager approval in Odoo.
  • BR-02 (Credit Hold Policy): Block SO confirmation automatically if client has unpaid invoices > 30 days overdue or debt > credit limit.
  • BR-03 (Automated Reorder Point): Reorder alert triggers when ATP stock drops below 100 reams for `ST-001`.
  • BR-04 (3-Way Matching Tolerance): Invoices matched against Delivery Note & PO within a 0.5% price variance tolerance.
🛡️ Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)
  • NFR-01 (Role-Based Access Control): Strict segregation of duties; Sales Reps cannot override price lists or approve vendor POs.
  • NFR-02 (Performance & Speed): Real-time ATP inventory lookups execute in < 1.0 second under 50 concurrent users.
  • NFR-03 (Scalability): Support catalog scaling to 1,000+ SKUs across multiple regional warehouses.
  • NFR-04 (Audit Trail & Integrity): Complete immutable change logs for all price updates, PO approvals, and credit overrides.
