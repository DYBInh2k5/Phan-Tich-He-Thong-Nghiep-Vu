Section 1 — Enterprise Context

Enterprise Profile & Wholesale Business Model

Company Profile & Operational Hubs

OfficePro Distribution Co., Ltd. is a regional B2B wholesale distributor supplying office stationery, commercial copy paper, and printer consumables across Southern Vietnam.

• Business Model: High-volume B2B wholesale procurement & contract fulfillment.

• Primary Upstream Suppliers: Double A, IK Plus, Thien Long, Pilot, HP Vietnam.

• Commercial Pricing Structure: Annual framework contract agreements with discounted tiers.

Central Warehouse at Tan Binh Industrial Park (2,500 m²; holds 80% palletized inventory).

Large commercial businesses ordering bulk monthly supplies. Demand customized contract pricing and Net 30 invoices.

Universities and schools ordering seasonal batches prior to academic semesters. Disbursements tied to formal state/institutional invoicing.

3. Stationery Retail Sub-Dealers (15% Revenue):
Local retail outlets purchasing frequent medium-sized reorders under tiered volume discount schedules.

B2B Contract Pricing Logic

Section 3 — Problem Identification

Primary Operational Problems & Quantified Friction

Problem 01: Contract Pricing Discrepancies

Individual reps maintain separate Excel files. Copy-pasting rates causes a 5–8% error rate on invoices, triggering client disputes and costing ~50 million VND monthly in invoice adjustments.

Problem 02: Post-Confirmation Stockouts

Sales confirms bulk orders based on outdated morning stock sheets. Receiving takes 24–48 hours to record, causing 3–4 stockouts per week post-confirmation and order cancellations.

Problem 03: Manual 3-Way Matching Overhead

Accounting expends 15–20 hours weekly manually reconciling paper SOs, Delivery Notes, and Invoices. Overdue accounts place orders without automatic blocks.

Diagnosed AS-IS Friction: Sales spreadsheet confusion, warehouse receiving delays, and accountant invoice matching overload.

Section 3 — Operational Bottlenecks

Departmental Bottlenecks & Operational Gaps

Departmental Friction Breakdown
  • • Sales Silo: Pricelists maintained in separate Excel files per rep; no central contract database.
  • • Warehouse Receiving Lag: Supplier trucks arrive, but paper receipts take 24–48 hours to enter into spreadsheets.
  • • Procurement Blindspot: Reorders triggered only after stock physically runs out; vendor RFQs handled over manual email chains (taking 2–3 days).
  • • Finance Reconciliation Bottleneck: Accounting manually compares physical delivery slips against printed orders to issue VAT invoices.
Manual vs Automated Journey: Moving from disconnected manual silos to a centralized digital ERP backbone.
Section 3 — Root Cause Analysis

5-Whys Root Cause Breakdown by Core Problem

Contract Pricing Errors
  1. Why 1? Sales Reps apply wrong unit prices. ➔ Copy-pasted from Excel.
  2. Why 2? Disconnected files? ➔ Each Rep maintains separate pricelists.
  3. Why 3? Separate files? ➔ Contracts filed as scanned PDFs.
  4. Why 4? Scanned PDFs? ➔ Lacks unified database mapping clients to contracts.
  5. Root Cause: Lack of centralized contract pricing engine enforcing rates automatically.
Post-Order Stockouts
  1. Why 1? Sales confirms orders out of stock. ➔ Stock checked via phone/morning sheet.
  2. Why 2? Why phone checks? ➔ Warehouse updates entered at end of day.
  3. Why 3? End of day? ➔ Goods receiving requires paper check (24–48h lag).
  4. Why 4? Manual check? ➔ Receiving not linked to PO in real time.
  5. Root Cause: Lack of real-time ATP inventory visibility engine across Sales & Warehouse.
Manual 3-Way Matching
  1. Why 1? Accountant spends 15–20h/wk. ➔ Delivery Notes and SOs compared manually.
  2. Why 2? Manual comparison? ➔ Delivery notes signed on paper by drivers.
  3. Why 3? Paper notes? ➔ No mobile warehouse app to confirm digital receipt.
  4. Why 4? No digital match? ➔ Invoicing and Sales systems operate in isolated silos.
  5. Root Cause: Lack of automated 3-way matching and hard credit hold control.
Section 3 — TO-BE Solution Design

TO-BE Integrated Business Process Re-engineering

1. Client Order Inquiry ➔ System Auto-Retrieves Contract Price
↓
2. Real-Time ATP Stock Check across Tan Binh & Bien Hoa Hubs
↓
3. Automated Reorder Trigger if stock < threshold (`ST-001`)
↓
4. Hard Credit Check (> 30 Days Overdue) ➔ Auto Order Confirmation
↓
5. Bin Location Picking ➔ Automated 3-Way Matching & VAT Invoice

Four Automation Pillars: Automated Contract Pricelists, Real-Time ATP Stock Visibility, Automated Reorder Thresholds, and Automated 3-Way Invoice Matching.

Target Digital Architecture: Seamless digital data streams synchronizing Sales, Real-Time ATP Warehouse, Supplier Restocking, and Finance.
