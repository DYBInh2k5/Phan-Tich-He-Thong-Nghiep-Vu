Company Profile & Market Position

OfficePro Distribution Co., Ltd. is a regional commercial B2B wholesale distributor supplying office stationery, commercial copy paper, and printer consumables to over 250 active corporate clients, educational institutions, and retail sub-dealers across Southern Vietnam.

Large commercial businesses ordering bulk monthly supplies. Require customized contracted pricing agreements, customized packing, and Net 30 invoices.

Universities and schools ordering seasonal batches prior to academic semesters. Disbursements tied to formal state/institutional invoicing.

3. Sub-Dealers & Stationery Retailers:
Local retail outlets purchasing frequent medium-sized reorders under tiered volume discount schedules.

Sales Channels: Direct B2B Account Representatives, Corporate Email/Zalo OA inquiries, and Formal Annual Framework Contracts.

Section 3 — Problem Identification

Primary Operational Problems & Business Friction

Problem 01: Pricing Errors

Contract Pricing Discrepancies (5–8% Error Rate): Individual Sales Reps store framework agreement prices in disconnected Excel files. Manual copy-pasting into client quotations results in 5–8% billing pricing discrepancies, causing invoice disputes and an estimated 50 million VND monthly adjustments.

Problem 02: Post-Order Stockouts

Post-Confirmation Stockouts (3–4 Orders/Week): Sales Reps confirm orders based on morning Excel stock dumps or phone calls to warehouse staff. Goods receiving takes 24–48 hours to update, leading to 3–4 stockouts per week post-confirmation and client cancellations.

Problem 03: Invoice & Credit Friction

Manual 3-Way Matching (15–20 Hours/Week): Accounting spends 15–20 hours/week manually cross-checking paper SOs, Delivery Notes, and Invoices. Net 30 credit hold policies are bypassable via informal verbal appeals, exposing the company to bad debt risk.

Section 3 — Root Cause Analysis

5-Whys Root Cause Breakdown by Core Problem

5-Whys: Contract Pricing Errors
  1. Why 1? Sales Reps apply wrong contract unit prices on orders. ➔ Prices copy-pasted manually from Excel files.
  2. Why 2? Why in disconnected Excel files? ➔ Each Rep maintains separate client pricelists.
  3. Why 3? Why separate files? ➔ Framework contracts filed as scanned PDFs in shared drives.
  4. Why 4? Why no central pricing engine? ➔ Lacks a unified database mapping clients to contracts.
  5. Why 5? (Root Cause): Lack of a centralized contract-based pricing engine that enforces contract price rules automatically.
5-Whys: Post-Order Stockouts
  1. Why 1? Sales confirms orders for items out of stock. ➔ Stock availability checked via phone call or daily Excel.
  2. Why 2? Why rely on phone/Excel? ➔ Warehouse stock updates entered in batch at end of day.
  3. Why 3? Why end of day? ➔ Goods receiving requires manual paper check (24-48h lag).
  4. Why 4? Why manual paper check? ➔ Inventory receiving not linked to PO in real time.
  5. Why 5? (Root Cause): Lack of real-time ATP inventory visibility engine connected across Sales & Warehouse.
5-Whys: Manual Invoice Matching
  1. Why 1? Accountant spends 15-20h/wk cross-checking invoices. ➔ Delivery Notes and SOs compared manually.
  2. Why 2? Why manual comparison? ➔ Delivery notes signed on paper by drivers upon delivery.
  3. Why 3? Why paper delivery notes? ➔ No mobile warehouse app to confirm digital delivery receipt.
  4. Why 4? Why no digital matching? ➔ Invoicing and Sales systems operate in isolated silos.
  5. Why 5? (Root Cause): Lack of automated 3-way matching and hard credit hold control.
