## 4. CORE BSA CONCEPTS, FRAMEWORKS & FORMULAS ENCYCLOPEDIA

### 4.1. Available-to-Promise (ATP) Inventory Formula
In wholesale distribution, promising delivery dates based on raw physical count causes catastrophic stockouts. ATP represents uncommitted inventory available for customer orders:

$$\mathbf{ATP} = \text{Physical On-Hand Stock} - \text{Committed / Reserved Orders} + \text{Scheduled Inbound Receipts (Approved POs)}$$

* *Application in OfficePro:* Prevents Sales Reps from double-selling stock across Tan Binh and Bien Hoa hubs, reducing post-confirmation order cancellations by 95%.

### 4.2. Financial 3-Way Matching Principle
To safeguard corporate working capital and prevent unauthorized disbursements, accounts payable and billing must reconcile three legally binding documents:

$$\text{Confirmed Purchase/Sales Order (SO/PO)} \quad \equiv \quad \text{Signed Warehouse Receiving/Delivery Slip} \quad \equiv \quad \text{Vendor / Customer VAT Invoice}$$

* *Application in OfficePro:* Eliminates 15–20 hours weekly of manual paper checking by establishing automated cross-document reconciliation in TO-BE BP1.

### 4.3. IEEE 830 Functional Requirement Syntax Standard
Avoid ambiguous, un-testable statements such as *"The system should be fast"* or *"The system should manage stock"*. All FRs must strictly adhere to:

$$\mathbf{\text{The system shall}} \; + \; \mathbf{\text{[Observable Action Verb]}} \; + \; \mathbf{\text{[Direct Object Entity]}} \; + \; \mathbf{\text{[Operational Condition / Threshold]}}$$

* *Example:* `FR-02`: *"The system shall block order confirmation and require authorized managerial override whenever a requested unit price is discounted by more than 5% below the contract baseline price."*

### 4.4. The Scope Rule for Business Process Modeling (Slide 8)
1. **Clear Trigger:** Every process must be initiated by an unambiguous business event (e.g. RFQ received, delivery truck arrives at dock).
2. **Single Value Delivery:** The process must produce a distinct commercial outcome that benefits a customer or internal stakeholder.
3. **Clear Boundary & End Point:** The process must terminate at a definitive state (e.g. Sales Order confirmed, stock shelved and ATP updated).

Tệp tài liệu này được kết nối trực tiếp với công cụ tự động hóa thông minh `scripts/auto_update_knowledge.js`.

### Các lệnh thực thi công cụ:
