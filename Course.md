**A.    ****Course Description:**

This course provides foundational knowledge of business systems analysis in the context of organizational information systems. It introduces how organizations operate, how business processes are supported by technology, and how business systems analysis contributes to technology implementation projects.

The course emphasizes core concepts and practical approaches in business and systems analysis without focusing on any specific analysis or design methodology. Students are introduced to business problems, stakeholder needs, and basic business and functional requirements that support the development and implementation of technology solutions.

Through project-based learning activities, students apply business systems analysis concepts within a simulated organizational environment. The course aims to strengthen students’ understanding of the relationship between business and technology and how business systems analysis contributes to technology implementation initiatives.

**B.     ****Course Objectives:**

| **CO** | **Course Objectives**                                                                                                                   |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CO1          | Provide students with a foundational understanding of enterprises, business processes, and enterprise information systems                     |
