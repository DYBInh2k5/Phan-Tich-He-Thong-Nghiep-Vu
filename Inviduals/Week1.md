### Task 1 — Investigation Focus
* **Primary Business Area**: B2B Sales Operations, Order Entry (Sales Orders - SO), and Contract-Based Pricing Management (Contract Pricing).

---

### Task 2 — Initial Understanding
* **What this area appears to do**: 
  * Receive RFQs and purchase requests from B2B customers (corporate clients, educational institutions, sub-dealers).
  * Verify active customer framework agreements and look up customer-specific contract prices.
  * Issue Quotations and generate Sales Orders (SO).
  * Hand over confirmed Sales Orders to Warehouse for fulfillment and Accounting for billing.
* **Who may be involved**:
  * Sales Manager, B2B Sales Executives, Sales Admins, Corporate B2B Clients.
* **What information may be needed**:
  * Active Customer Contracts Master Data.
  * Customer-specific Contract Price Lists (Pricelists).
  * Real-time Available-to-Promise (ATP) Inventory from Warehouse.
  * Customer Credit Limits and Outstanding Overdue Debt Status from Accounting.

---

### Task 3 — Stakeholder
* **Primary Stakeholder**: **Sales Manager** / **Senior B2B Sales Executive**.

---

### Task 4 — Interview Questions (Week 2 Interview Preparation)
1. How do sales representatives currently receive and process incoming B2B purchase orders from corporate clients step-by-step?
2. Where are customer-specific contract price agreements currently stored and retrieved (e.g., Excel sheets, emails, or physical files)?
3. How does a sales representative verify whether a product (e.g., A4 Copy Paper Double A 70gsm) has sufficient stock before confirming an order to a customer?
4. What is the formal approval process when a client requests a price override or a custom discount outside of the signed contract agreement?
5. How do sales staff check if an ordering customer has an overdue balance or exceeded credit limit before confirming a new sales order?
6. Once a Sales Order is confirmed, how is order information handed over to the Warehouse and Accounting departments?
7. What are the most common customer complaints or operational friction points regarding B2B sales orders, and what causes them?

---

### Task 5 — Initial Assumptions (To be verified during interview)
* **Assumption 1**: Sales representatives currently look up contract prices manually from disconnected Excel files, resulting in occasional pricing errors on customer invoices.
* **Assumption 2**: Sales staff confirm delivery timelines to clients based on informal verbal checks with the warehouse rather than accessing real-time inventory availability.
* **Assumption 3**: There is currently no automated system safeguard to block new sales order generation for clients with debt overdue by more than 30 days.
